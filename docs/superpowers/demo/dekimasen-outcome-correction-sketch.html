Outcome correction — UI sketch

Concert page round rows only. Press Change on any row below and it returns to unrecorded — the existing capture buttons come back and you re-answer. Nothing here is a new answer button; the whole feature is one un-answer. Toggle Option A / B to compare the two treatments.

1 · Today — the dead ends

Three real rows as the app renders them right now. Every one of them is terminal: capture_gates opens the result buttons only from APPLIED, so a settled round falls through _capture_actions.html to its last two branches.

What the press does: deletes this user’s RoundOutcome row (and any RoundOutcomeDay rows) for the round, then re-syncs their rules exactly as every other outcome write does. The round goes back to “no row”, which is the common case the whole model is already built around — so every downstream reader (board column, Coming up, .ics feed, Discover pill) is correct with no change to any of them.

3 · Clearing a ticket you hold

Clearing Lost or Not applying costs nothing — press and re-answer. Clearing Won or Secured throws away the record that you hold a ticket, and the app already has a house rule for that: the per-leg opt-out gates the same loss behind a heavy confirmation naming what goes. Same shape here (press “Change” on the Secured row above to see it).

Clear your answer for this round?

You recorded a ticket for プレイガイド先行. Clearing it removes:

  • your Secured record for this round
  • the payment reminder that record is holding open
  • the reason other rounds on this day are marked Covered

The round goes back to unrecorded and you can answer it again. Nothing else on the concert changes.

4 · Per leg — one round, two nights

One lottery covering Saturday and Sunday, won on Saturday and lost on Sunday. The round renders under each leg and the buttons are already scoped to that leg (only_day), so Change is too: fixing Sunday leaves Saturday’s ticket alone. Press Change under Sunday.

Re-derivation after a per-leg clear: delete that leg’s row, then read the round off what survives — any WON row left → the round stays WON (and PAID stays PAID); all remaining rows LOST with nothing open → LOST; some leg now unanswered → back to APPLIED, which is honest (you had a result, so you were in the draw) and is exactly the state the won/lost buttons re-open from; no rows left at all → the round outcome is cleared too.

Rejected treatment: hover-only reveal. It keeps settled rows perfectly clean, but touch has no hover and mobile is a retrofit here — the correction would be unreachable on a phone, which is where the mis-press happens in the first place.
