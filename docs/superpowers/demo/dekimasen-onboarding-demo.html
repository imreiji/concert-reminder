Privacy policy

Last updated 19 July 2026

dekimasen.app tracks Japanese concert ticketing deadlines and reminds you about them on Discord. This page explains what we store and why. The short version: we keep the minimum needed to send you the right reminder at the right time.

What we collect

  • Your Discord identity — your Discord user ID and username, received when you sign in with Discord. We use it to address your DMs and to know which board is yours.
  • Your choices — the tags you follow, reminder presets, per-concert overrides, your timezone, and the lottery outcomes you record (applied, won, lost, paid).
  • Delivery state — which reminders we have sent, so we do not send them twice.

What we do not collect

We do not read your Discord messages, servers, or contacts. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The only cookie we set is your login session, which is a revocable token — we store only its SHA-256 hash, never the raw value.

Your calendar feed

If you generate a calendar feed, its link contains a secret token. Anyone with the link can read your deadlines, so treat it like a password. We store only the hash of the token; if you lose the link the fix is to generate a new one, which immediately revokes the old.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. Concerts, rounds and tags you create as an editor go into a catalogue shared with all users — but those are event facts, not personal information. Your follows, outcomes and reminders are never shown to anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from Preferences at any time. That removes your subscriptions, reminders, presets, outcomes and sessions. Concerts and tags you contributed stay in the shared catalogue with your name removed.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach the maintainer on Discord, or open an issue on the project repository.

Terms of service

Last updated 19 July 2026

What the service does

dekimasen.app records ticketing deadlines for Japanese concerts and sends reminders on Discord and via an optional calendar feed. Deadline data is entered by editors and parsed from public pages. It can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date.

No guarantee

  • We do not sell tickets and are not affiliated with any artist, promoter, fan club, or ticketing platform.
  • Reminders are best-effort. A missed DM, a wrong time, or a deadline that changed after we recorded it can happen. Always confirm times on the official page.
  • You are responsible for actually entering lotteries and completing payments. We track where you stand; we do not act on your behalf.

Acceptable use

Editors may add and change concerts, rounds and tags for everyone. Do not enter false or abusive data, and do not use the service to scrape or overload ticketing sites. We may revoke editor access or accounts that misuse the shared catalogue.

Availability

The service is provided "as is", with no uptime guarantee. It may go down, lose data, or be discontinued. Keep your own note of any deadline you truly cannot miss.
