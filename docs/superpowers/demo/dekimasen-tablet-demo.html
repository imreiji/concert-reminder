Tablet band (701–1040 px) — the target states, one framed panel each

Five fixed-width panels at the band's reference widths (820 and 1000 px), on the shipped design tokens (this page follows your light/dark setting, like the app). Each panel proves one decision from the spec:

  • Compact header — one row, never wraps: nowrap wordmark (no secondary), nav, then theme + language + add + icon-only Preferences/Sign out. Shown in EN and the widest-language ja case.
  • Swipeable 4-column board — all four columns at full card width, real horizontal scroll with snap, the next column's edge peeking. Try it: the board below actually scrolls.
  • Discover under the filter sheet — the sidebar folds into a Filters disclosure above the grid; tiles auto-fill 2-across in-band.
  • Discover at 1000 px — same sheet, tiles 3-across, proving the upper edge of the band.
  • Coming-up rows — the "what happens" text folds into the title's small line (data-happens) rather than being dropped; capture buttons keep their context.
