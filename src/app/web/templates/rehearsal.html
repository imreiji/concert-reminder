Rehearsal

Local harness. Seeds one concert, pulls its reminders forward so the real 60-second tick delivers them now, and sends every DM shape on demand.

Actions

Each press redirects back here, so the table below always shows what is true now. Nothing is faked but the wait: the planner computed these rows and the real send path delivers them.

The prescribed walk

Order is load-bearing. Payment only offers Paid from Won, so step 3 must come before 4 and 4 before 6 — press Lost at step 4 and the ladder ends there.

  1. Start — a new-event notice, your default preset auto-applied. Buttons: apply / remove / deadlines.
  2. Next reminder — the 1st lottery opens. Button: snooze.
  3. Next reminder — the 1st lottery closes; press I applied. Buttons: applied / notapplied / remindlater.
  4. Next reminder — its results; press Won. That round covers both legs, so the DM asks leg by leg: wonall / one wonday per leg / lostall.
  5. Observe — the upgrade round becomes eligible, and the general sale goes quiet. A round that stops arriving is the hardest thing to notice by hand.
  6. Next reminder — payment is due; press Paid. Button: paid.
  7. Next reminder — Day 1 starts. Button: snooze.
  8. Cancel the show — a leg-cancelled notice; press Turn my reminders back on. Button: reinstate. This step is terminal. It cancels every live leg at once — the notice is concert-scoped and stays silent while any live reminder remains — so it clears every queue row left, the upgrade round's included. Press it early and the walk is over.
  9. End — the concert is deleted and the cascades take the rest. Your user, presets and subscriptions are untouched.
