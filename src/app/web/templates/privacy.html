Privacy policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

dekimasen.app tracks deadlines for Japanese concerts and sends you a Discord DM before the ones you care about close. To do that it has to store a small amount of information about you. This page says exactly what, why, and for how long, in plain language. The rules for using the service itself are on a separate page — see the terms of service.

What we collect

  • Your Discord account basics — your Discord user ID, your display name, and your avatar hash. We ask Discord for the identify scope only, which is the minimum available.
  • Your timezone — either the one you picked, or the one your browser reported, so deadlines show in your local time alongside JST.
  • Your reminder settings — the reminder rules you create, your reminder presets, the tags you subscribe to, and any lottery outcomes you record (applied, won, lost, paid), which the app uses to stop reminding you about rounds you are done with.
  • Pending and sent reminders — the queue of reminders scheduled for you, and whether each has been delivered.
  • Login sessions — a random session token per login. Only its SHA-256 hash is stored, never the token itself.
  • A calendar feed token, if you generate one. Also stored only as a SHA-256 hash. Anyone holding the raw feed URL can read your upcoming deadlines, so treat it as a password; if you lose it or want to revoke it, generate a new one and the old URL stops working.
  • Whether your DMs are reachable — a single flag, set when Discord refuses a DM, so the site can tell you why reminders stopped arriving.
  • What you contributed — if you have editor rights, the concerts, tags, and edits you made are recorded with you as the author.

What we do not collect

  • No email address. Discord login never hands us one.
  • No IP addresses in the database.
  • No message content. The bot uses no privileged intents, so it cannot read your messages, your server list, or your friends.
  • No analytics, no third-party trackers, no advertising.

Why we collect it

Solely to send you the concert-deadline reminders you asked for, and to show your own settings back to you. There is no other use. Nothing here is profiled, scored, or used to make automated decisions about you.

Sharing

We share nothing. Your data is not sold, rented, or given to third parties. The only outbound traffic carrying anything about you is the Discord API call that delivers your DM, which is the service you signed up for.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. The database is backed up nightly to a private, non-public storage bucket (encrypted at rest by the storage provider), and those backups expire automatically after 30 days. So after a deletion, your data is gone from the live site immediately but can persist in backups for up to 30 more days. We would rather say that than claim instant erasure.

Cookies

One cookie: your login session. It is strictly necessary — without it you cannot stay signed in. There are no analytics or tracking cookies, which is why you are not being asked to click through a consent banner.

Deleting your account removes everything personal listed above: your account row, your sessions, your reminder rules, presets and preset items, your tag subscriptions, your recorded lottery outcomes, your queued reminders, and your pending notifications. What stays is the shared catalogue — concerts, tags, and edit history you contributed — with the author field blanked out, so the events everyone else is following do not disappear because one person left.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify, since we do not have your email address.
